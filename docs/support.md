# Support — JSON Resume Connect

## Contact

Email **[ismail@kattakath.com](mailto:ismail@kattakath.com?subject=JSON%20Resume%20Connect%20support)**.

- Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- Copy the error message exactly if the step showed one.
- **Do not send your résumé, a password or a sign-in code.** They are not needed to help you.

This is a free add-on maintained by one person. Replies are best effort, usually within a few
days.

## Common questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The step says my résumé is invalid | Open your résumé in the [jsonresume.org](https://jsonresume.org/) editor, fix the field the message names, save, and run the flow again |
| The step says my résumé has no email | Add an email address to the résumé's basics in the jsonresume.org editor |
| How do I disconnect my account? | Open the step in your flow and click **Disconnect** |
| How do I remove the add-on? | Uninstall it from the Google Workspace Marketplace, then remove its access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) |
| What data does it keep? | See the [privacy policy](../privacy/) |
| I cannot sign in to jsonresume.org | That account belongs to jsonresume.org. Ask at [jsonresume/jsonresume.org](https://github.com/jsonresume/jsonresume.org/issues) |

## For developers

Bug reports and feature requests are also welcome on

security problem privately, as described in the
[security policy](https://github.com/ismailkattakath/jsonresume-connect/security/policy).
